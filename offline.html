Connection Lost

Nightlight can't reach the server

Your internet connection is working, but the launcher service can't be contacted right now. Join the Discord for live status, or check the fixes below.

Common Problems
DNS problem — can't resolve nl.onajlikezz.xyz
Your router or ISP is refusing to translate the domain name into a server address. This is the most common reason the launcher suddenly stops connecting on an otherwise working network.
1
Turn on a VPN. Even a free one like ProtonVPN or Cloudflare WARP routes your DNS through a different resolver and usually fixes it instantly.
2
Use your phone's hotspot. Turn on mobile data on your phone, enable the hotspot, and connect the PC to it. This bypasses your home network's DNS entirely. Once the launcher loads, switch back to your normal Wi-Fi.
3
Change your PC's DNS to 1.1.1.1 and 1.0.0.1 (Cloudflare) or 8.8.8.8 (Google), then run ipconfig /flushdns in Command Prompt.
Firewall or antivirus is blocking the launcher
Windows Defender, third-party antivirus, or a corporate firewall may be silently blocking outbound HTTPS traffic from Electron.
•
Open Windows Security → Firewall & network protection → Allow an app through firewall and add Nightlight Launcher for both Private and Public networks.
•
Temporarily disable third-party antivirus (Avast, Kaspersky, Malwarebytes) and retry. If it works, add an exclusion for the launcher folder.
•
On a work or school network? Try a personal connection — many block non-standard traffic by policy.
The server itself is down or under maintenance
If everyone is affected and Discord is quiet, it's usually our side. We post status updates and ETA in the announcement channel.
•
Check the #announcements channel in the Discord server — we always post before restarts.
•
Try again in 5–10 minutes. Most outages are short.
System clock is out of sync
If your computer's date or time is off by more than a few minutes, HTTPS handshakes fail and the launcher can't reach any server at all.
•
Right-click the clock → Adjust date and time → turn on Set time automatically and Set time zone automatically.
•
Then click Sync now and retry the launcher.
